Data Products

OOI Data products, and their different levels, are defined for individual variables (or parameters) within a dataset, not to the dataset as a whole. A single dataset may contain 0-N variables defined as an OOI data product. These may exist at multiple processing levels simultaneously reflecting the different transformations applied to each measured or derived quantity. The data product levels defined by OOI are:

  • L0 -- Raw instrument output exactly as recorded by the sensor, in whatever units the instrument natively produces (counts, volts, or other unprocessed units). L0 values have no calibration or correction applied and are usually not directly interpretable as physical quantities.

  • L1 -- Computed from L0 inputs from one instrument, together with calibration coefficients and metadata such as position and time; or an instrument-computed value that has been rescaled or corrected (e.g., for magnetic declination). L1 variables may be reported directly by the instrument (onboard calculation), calculated by ION Functions, or both.

  • L2 -- A derived value, computed with at least one or more L1 or L2 product as an input. The inputs may come from the same instrument (e.g., practical salinity, PRACSAL_L2, from the CTD's own conductivity, temperature, and pressure) or from another instrument (e.g., dissolved oxygen, DOXYGEN_L2, corrected using salinity from a co-located CTD).
